Teaching Method

A five-verse rhythm helps the teacher slow down, protect the context, and let Scripture lead instead of rushing through too much at once.

Why teach around five verses at a time?

  • It keeps the passage manageable. People can follow the text without feeling overwhelmed.
  • It protects context. You can explain what came before, what is happening now, and where the passage is going.
  • It gives room for observation. Repeated words, commands, promises, warnings, and Christ-centered truths become easier to see.
  • It helps application become clear. The goal is not just information, but faithful response to God’s Word.
  • It leaves space for prayer. The teacher can slow down and ask the Lord for wisdom, humility, and obedience.

Simple teaching rhythm:

  • Read the selected verses slowly and clearly.
  • Explain the context in plain language.
  • Mark key words, repeated ideas, promises, warnings, or commands.
  • Point people to Christ and the Gospel.
  • Give one clear application or prayerful response.
The five-verse number is a guide, not a law. Some passages may need fewer verses. Some may allow more. The goal is faithful, Spirit-led clarity.

I.R.O.N. Study Prompts

I — Identify the Truth
What does this passage clearly teach about God, Christ, salvation, faith, obedience, or Christian living?

R — Reflect on the Meaning
Why would the original audience have needed this message? What is the Holy Spirit emphasizing here?

O — Observe the Details
Are there repeated words, commands, warnings, promises, contrasts, examples, or connections to other Scripture?

N — Next Faithful Step
Based on this passage, what should I believe, repent of, obey, trust, remember, or do today?

Lord Jesus, help me rightly understand Your Word and faithfully respond to what You are showing me.

Prayerful Response

Ask the Lord:

• What truth do You want me to see clearly?
• What do I need to believe, surrender, repent of, obey, or share?
• How should this passage shape my heart, my words, and my steps today?

Holy Spirit, give me wisdom, humility, discernment, and obedience through the Word of God.

Study Reminder

  • Read the passage slowly and prayerfully.
  • Stay rooted in the surrounding context.
  • Let Scripture interpret Scripture.
  • Look for Christ, the Gospel, commands, warnings, promises, and application.
  • Move from understanding to faithful obedience.
