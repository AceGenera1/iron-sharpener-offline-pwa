Iron Sharpener is built around the I.R.O.N. Study Method, a Christ-centered approach to biblical discipleship.

I – Investigate the Scriptures
What does God's Word say?

R – Reflect on the Meaning
What did the text mean, and what truth is God revealing?

O – Obey the Truth
How should this shape my life, faith, and walk with Christ?

N – Navigate the Mission
How does this equip me to follow Christ and serve others?

The goal of Iron Sharpener is not merely to increase knowledge, but to help believers grow in Christ, walk in the power of the Holy Spirit, and faithfully live out God's Word.
