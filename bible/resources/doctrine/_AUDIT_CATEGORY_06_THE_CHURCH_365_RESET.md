## Overlap / Distinctness Notes

- The Nature of the Church, Body of Christ, Bride of Christ, Household of God, and People of God are intentionally distinct images of the church.
- Church Discipline and Restoration in the Church are separated: discipline focuses on correction of sin; restoration focuses on the repentant return/healing process.
- Baptism and the Lord’s Supper are separated as distinct ordinances.
- Mission of the Church and Great Commission are separated: mission is broad witness; Great Commission is Christ’s direct disciple-making command.
- Pastoral Care and Church Leadership are separated: leadership covers qualified offices/responsibility; pastoral care covers soul care/shepherding practice.
