## Category 3 distinctness check

Special attention was given to keeping close Christology doctrines distinct:

- Eternal Son of God — Christ’s preexistence and eternal Sonship
- Deity of Christ — Christ is truly God and worthy of worship
- Humanity of Christ — Christ truly became man and entered real human weakness without sin
- Incarnation of Christ — the eternal Son took flesh without ceasing to be God
- Atonement of Christ — the broad saving work by which Christ deals with sin and reconciles sinners to God
- Substitutionary Death of Christ — Christ specifically died in the place of sinners
- Blood of Christ — Christ’s sacrificial death securing cleansing, forgiveness, redemption, and covenant mercy
- Cross of Christ — the saving, shame-bearing, victorious death of Christ at Calvary
- Intercession of Christ — Christ’s ongoing priestly representation for His people
- Advocacy of Christ — Christ’s righteous representation when believers sin
